# Warranties

The warranties a person keeps on Home: adding, archiving and importing them, opening their documents, and the claims made against them.

## Boundary

Owns warranties as Home presents them, their documents and their claims. Does not own the status dashboard's statistics, a person's settings or reminder schedule, or anything in the admin settings.
